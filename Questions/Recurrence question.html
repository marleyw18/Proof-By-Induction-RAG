Suppose that $g: \mathbb{N} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ is defined by
  • $g(0)=0$
  • $g(1)=\frac{4}{3}$
  • $g(n)=\frac{4}{3}g(n-1) - \frac{1}{3}g(n-2)$, for $n\geq 2$.
Use induction to prove that $g(n) = 2 - \frac{2}{3^n}$ for any natural number $n$. {{#feedback.manual}}
